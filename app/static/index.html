ESPN login codes

How to get them (about 2 minutes, needs a computer once)
  1. On any computer, open Chrome and log in at fantasy.espn.com.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click → Inspect). Click the Application tab (it may be behind »).
  3. On the left: Storage → Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com.
  4. Find espn_s2, click it, and copy the long Cookie Value shown at the bottom. Paste below.
  5. Do the same for SWID (looks like {ABCD1234-…}).
  6. Tap Save. They usually last about a year. If they stop working you'll get an alert.

They're stored only on your NAS. They let the bot act as you on ESPN, so keep this page's password on.
